Hogwarts Castle

Alnwick castle in Northumberland, England, is considered the primary "real-life" Hogwarts, used for exterior scenes in the first two Harry Potter films. Known for the spot where Harry had his first flying lesson, it offers visitor activities like broomstick training. Other key locations include Durham Cathedral and Oxford’s New College
